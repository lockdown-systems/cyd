# Derive Bluesky session holders from account state

Cyd holds one Bluesky OAuth session per DID, shared by every platform that acts on that identity, and decides when to revoke it by deriving its holders — an X account with its Bluesky migration connected, or a connected Bluesky local account bound to that DID — from account state on every question, rather than by maintaining a reference count beside the session. Releasing a hold means making the releasing account's own state say it is disconnected and then asking again who is left: while anyone is, the session is untouched and no other account is signed out; when nobody is, Cyd revokes at the PDS and destroys the local session and DPoP key material. A counter was rejected because a crash or a forced quit can leave it one too high, stranding a session nothing can revoke, or one too low after a partial delete, signing out an account that is still connected; account state cannot drift from itself, and a session left with zero holders by interrupted work is swept and revoked at the next startup. No account can force-revoke a session another account still uses, and no interface offers to.
